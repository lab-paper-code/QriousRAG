Nadine Coyle was born on June 15, 1985 in Derry, Northern Ireland as Nadine Elizabeth Louise Coyle.
Trade Mark (2)
She was the third girl to make it into Girls Aloud .
Ranked #35 in FHM 100 Sexiest Women 2005.
Member of the all girl pop group Girls Aloud from the UK.
Supporter of Glasgow Celtic Football Club.
Has won two series of Pop Idol (2001), the first was in Ireland but she was disqualified when the producers found out she was under the minimum age.
Lives in the US.
Touring the world, and releasing a single with Girls Aloud in March 2006. [February 2006]
Was in relationship with Jason Bell from 2008 to 2011 and then they got back together in 2013. They have a daughter together.
Gave birth to her 1st child at age 28, a daughter Anaíya Bell on February 10, 2014. Child's father is her fiancé, Jason Bell.
