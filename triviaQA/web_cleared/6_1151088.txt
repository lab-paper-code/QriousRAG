When Alexander Graham Bell got the patent for the telephone and other facts
Additional Facts
The telephone was a culmination of work done by many individuals, sharing and borrowing each other's ideas
Edinburgh-born Alexander Graham Bell patented his telephone model just hours before a rival inventor
Telephone came about through the discovery that a thin metal sheet vibrating in an electromagnetic field produces an electrical waveform that corresponds to the vibration and can be acoustically reproduced
Bell may have made the first call to his assistant in the next room 'Mr Watson, come here - I want to see you'
They first publicly demonstrated their invention at the Centennial Exhibition in Philadelphia in 1876
A month later, they made their first long-distance call - over 10 miles - between their respective homes in Canada
In 1877, Bell set up the Bell Telephone Company, and 150,000 households in the US had telephones within a decade
Bell's industrious mind was also responsible for the loudspeaker (1876) and the forerunner of the metal detector (1881)
