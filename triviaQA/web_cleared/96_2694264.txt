1 of 8The marrying kind
Taylor loved and loved hard, marrying eight times to seven different men: Conrad Hilton, Michael Wilding, Michael Todd, Eddie Fisher, Richard Burton (twice), John Warner and Larry Fortensky. The marriages spanned 46 years and two of them were born from affairs. Only one didn't end in divorce: her third marriage to film producer Todd, who died in a plane crash.
