Tributes to Kenneth Wolstenholme
Legendary BBC sports commentator Kenneth Wolstenholme dies aged 81.
E-mail your tributes.
For more than 20 years Wolstenholme was the voice of football on television, commentating on the FA Cup final, World Cup and internationals.
He will be remembered by most for uttering the words "they think it's all over, it is now" during England's 1966 World Cup win at Wembley.
Wolstenholme left the BBC in 1971 and went on to work for other media organisations.
