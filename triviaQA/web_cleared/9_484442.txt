The highest-read news and media website – with over 7 billion readers per month – was founded by Jerry Yang and David Filo in January 1994, as a “hierarchical web portal”, and was originally named “Jerry and David’s guide to the World Wide Web”. It was renamed “Yahoo!” – “Yet Another Hierarchically Organized Oracle” – in March 1994, and the domain was created on January 18, 1995. The company’s 1996 homepage had a simple search bar and hyperlinks to other websites, and was very straightforward in its design.
