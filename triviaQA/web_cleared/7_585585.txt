CHRONOLOGY OF THE CAPTAIN SCARLET UNIVERSE
The following chronology, which could be stated as "semi-official", and compiled by Keith Ansell, uses dates and information  taken not only from the Captain Scarlet Universe, but from some other universes  created for the many Gerry Anderson's TV series.  Anderson himself did not  conceive the series so they should intertwine with each other's, like it often  happens in the "TV Century 21" books, although  some ideas, such as a World  Government, are common to many of these series. 
 
The Chronology first  appeared in the pages of  "Century 21 - The World of Gerry Anderson",  issue 15, published in 1993. 
Ideas taken from other universes are marked by a  yellow star, with references reported  at the bottom of this page. 
If someone happens to notice mistakes, errors or omissions, feel free to inform me, so I could update this page accordingly. 
 
A Chronology of the Gerry Anderson  Universe , using all the Anderson series, and also compiled by Keith Ansell can be found on this website.  (Added February 10, 2014)
