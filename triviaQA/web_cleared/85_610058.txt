Choir
The five hundred year old Choir consists of 16 boys and 12 men, of whom half are Lay Clerks and half undergraduates. The boys attend Christ Church Cathedral School, a prep school, as boarders. The Choir is 'famed for the youthfulness of its sound and its daring and adventurous musical programming.'

Building
Founded as Cardinal College in 1524, Christ Church took over the site of St Frideswide's Monastery which dated back to the 9th century. After 1546 Henry VIII appointed the old monastery church as Oxford's Cathedral. It is a Romanesque gem, being constructed at the end of the 12th century and surviving the dissolution. Great Tom, the 1682 bell tower was designed by Sir Christopher Wren.
