A 1983 off-air recording of the long-running and classic Thames Television situation comedy 'Never The Twain' starring Donald Sinden and Windsor Davies as the warring antique dealers Simon Peel and Oliver Smallbridge. Never The Twain ran for eleven series from 7 September 1981 to 9 October 1991. It was created by Johnnie Mortimer, and was the only sitcom he ever created without his usual writing partner, Brian Cooke. Mortimer wrote the entirety of the first two series, one episode of the seventh, and five out of six episodes of the eighth, with the rest being mainly written by John Kane and Vince Powell (who wrote the whole of the last three series). RIP Donald Sinden.
