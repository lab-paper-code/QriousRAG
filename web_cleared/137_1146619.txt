By Andre M. on December 2, 2016
Format: DVD|Verified Purchase
Those who may dislike silent films may change their minds after seeing this one.
This retelling of the classic novel is actually the second, not fist filmed version as commonly believed (the original was a crude early silent from 1907 that is of historical interest only). This version is a well told drama full of thrills and excitement about the Jewish wealthy heir who faces treachery from his childhood friend and forced into slavery. Much has been said about the story, but it is really well told here. Great acting, chariot races, ship battles, and even a few scenes in early technicolor. In some ways, it precedes KING OF KINGS two years later in 1927 with the color scenes, high production quality, and the Jesus Christ aspects. It is so exciting and well done that as is the case with KING OF KINGS, it may convert haters of silent cinema into fans of the genre.
